Delete your account

Last updated: 27 September 2026

You can delete your Ambient account and all associated data at any time, directly from the app or by emailing us. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Important: Deleting your account removes your synced tasks and routines from our servers. Plans saved on your device are not deleted automatically — you can remove them separately from Settings → Delete all my data. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription; manage that through your Google Play or App Store account settings.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

This is the fastest method and takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Ambient and go to Settings (tap the gear icon).

  2. Tap Account.

  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.

  4. Confirm the deletion in the dialog. Your account and all synced data are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email us from the address associated with your account. We will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Email us to delete your account

What gets deleted

  • Your account credentials (email address, encrypted password, account ID)
  • All tasks and routines synced to your account
  • Consent records tied to your account

Deletion is applied immediately to live data. Encrypted backups are purged within 30 days. Purchase records are retained by RevenueCat, Apple, and Google as required by law for tax and accounting purposes.
