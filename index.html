    Questions

    What is Ambient?

    Ambient is a visual day planner app for iPhone and Android, designed for people with ADHD and other neurodivergent adults. Instead of waiting to be opened, it shows your plan on your home screen (widgets), in your notifications (reminders that can nudge again) and as one big focus timer.

    Who is Ambient for?

    People who find traditional planners easy to forget — especially ADHD and neurodivergent adults who struggle with time blindness, getting started, or too many tasks at once.

    How is Ambient different from other planner apps?

    Most planners only help if you remember to open them. Ambient brings the plan to you: home-screen widgets show what is now and next, reminders can nudge you again if you have not started, and a brain dump turns whatever you type or say into a timed plan.

    When will Ambient launch, and on which phones?

    Ambient is coming soon to iPhone (iOS) and Android. Join the beta on this page to get an invite before the public launch.

    Is Ambient free?

    Ambient will have a free plan. Some features, such as more routines and syncing across devices, are part of an optional Ambient Pro upgrade.

    Is Ambient a medical app?

    No. Ambient is a planning tool that supports executive function. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat ADHD or any other condition.

    What happens to my data?

    Your plans are stored on your phone. They leave your device only if you choose to sync them to your account. There are no ads and no data selling. See the Privacy Policy for details.
